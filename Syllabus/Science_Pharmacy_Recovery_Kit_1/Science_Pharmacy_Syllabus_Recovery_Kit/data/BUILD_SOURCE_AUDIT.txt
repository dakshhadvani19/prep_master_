Interpretation safeguards
------------------------
1. Course/subject IDs read from mockData.js are source catalog IDs. They are not assumed to be Supabase numeric IDs.
2. Duplicate title groups are searched once to reduce redundant downloads, but each occurrence is retained in the occurrence report and cross-course match issues are flagged.
3. PDFs from fallback institutions are labelled comparable references, not Marwadi University syllabi.
4. A network/processing failure is not evidence that a syllabus does not exist.
5. No Supabase, GitHub, or website writes are performed by the runner.
