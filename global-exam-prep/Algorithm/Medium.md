# Difficulty Overlay — Medium

Append these instructions to exactly one selected exam prompt.

## Intended cognitive demand
Require meaningful application, interpretation, comparison, or two connected reasoning steps. A question may integrate two related topics when that integration is natural for the syllabus.

## Construction rules
- Use a scenario, calculation, code trace, comparison or applied task only when it adds a genuine reasoning requirement.
- Provide the data, assumptions and constraints needed to determine the answer.
- For MCQs, use plausible distractors reflecting common mistakes, incomplete answers, nearby concepts or a valid rule applied under the wrong condition.
- Ensure one answer is uniquely defensible; the distinguishing condition must be present in the stem.
- For theory questions, match the depth and breadth to the assigned marks and include a rubric that awards credit for the required reasoning.
- Do not make a recall question appear harder merely by wrapping it in a long story.
- Stay within the verified syllabus and exam-specific blueprint.
