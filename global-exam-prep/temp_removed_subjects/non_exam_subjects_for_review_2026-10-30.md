## Recommended treatment

- **Internships, placements, attachments and Practice School:** use verified learning outcomes, attendance/engagement where prescribed, logbook, employer/mentor evaluation, report and viva. Do not generate ordinary MCQ-only papers for the placement itself.
- **Seminars and colloquia:** presentation rubric, subject understanding, source quality, discussion and viva. A short theory component is optional only if the official assessment scheme requires it.
- **Moot Court Training:** oral advocacy, legal research, memorial/drafting work and performance rubric, not a conventional MCQ paper as the sole assessment.
- **NCC and physical education / yoga / wellness:** activity completion and practical performance/participation records. Keep any official written component if the applicable scheme prescribes one.
- **Attachment:** portfolio, supervisor feedback, field report and viva, subject to the official course rubric.
